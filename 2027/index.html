MMVE 2027

March 30th – April 2nd 2027 — Ghent, Belgium

Welcome to MMVE 2027

The International Workshop on Immersive Mixed and Virtual Environment Systems (MMVE 2027) will take place in person in Ghent, Belgium, co-located with the ACM Multimedia Systems Conference (MMSys 2027), which runs from March 30th to April 2nd 2027. MMVE provides a discussion-focused forum between researchers and practitioners to exchange ideas, present advances in immersive media systems, and shape the future of XR research.

About MMVE

Since 2018, MMVE has evolved into a vibrant platform bridging XR technology, experience design, perception research, and systems engineering. The workshop welcomes contributions on immersive multimedia systems, user experience, performance and quality evaluation, accessibility, and emerging interaction paradigms enabling virtual, augmented, and mixed reality.

Topics of Interest

This year's theme is Accountability in Immersive Tech; see the Call for Papers for details. Topics include:

  • Ethics & Society in XR
  • Immersive & Interactive Content
  • Quality & Performance
  • Accessibility & Inclusivity
  • Support for Immersive Systems
  • Perception & Behaviour
  • Applied Contexts

See the Call for Papers for the full list of topics and submission instructions.

We look forward to welcoming you in Ghent at MMVE 2027!
